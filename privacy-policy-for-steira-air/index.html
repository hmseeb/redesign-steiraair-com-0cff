Privacy Policy

How Steira Air collects, uses and protects the information you share with us through this website.

Get a free quote Call +1 470-539-8969

This Privacy Policy explains how Steira Air (“Steira Air”, “we”, “us”) handles information collected through this website. If anything here is unclear, call us on +1 470-539-8969 during business hours (Monday – Saturday, 8AM – 5PM).

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you submit a quote request or contact form, that typically includes your name, email address, phone number, property type, the service you are interested in, your service location, a preferred date and any message you write. We also record the page address the form was submitted from so we can respond in context.

We do not ask for payment details, government identifiers or health information through this website, and the site does not include file upload fields.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote or scope of work.
  • To schedule and deliver the services you request.
  • To follow up about work we have carried out for you.
  • To keep our own records of enquiries and jobs.

We do not sell your personal information.

How form submissions are handled

Form submissions on this website are transmitted to our lead management provider, which stores them and notifies our team so we can reply. That provider processes the information on our behalf and only for the purpose of delivering enquiries to us.

Third-party links and services

Some links take you to services outside this website — for example our online booking calendar, Google Maps, or our profiles on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. Once you follow those links, the privacy policy of that provider applies to the information you share there.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our business and legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Call +1 470-539-8969 or use our contact form and we will take care of it.

Children

This website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the one that applies.
