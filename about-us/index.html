Understanding Indoor Air Quality (IAQ)

Indoor Air Quality refers to the condition of the air within buildings and structures, especially as it relates to the health and comfort of building occupants. Poor IAQ can lead to various health issues, including respiratory problems, allergies, and other serious illnesses.

Factors affecting IAQ include pollutants such as dust, mold, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). Steira Air employs state-of-the-art monitoring systems and cleaning technologies to ensure that indoor environments remain safe and conducive to health.

Benefits of regular HVAC maintenance

Regular HVAC maintenance is crucial for ensuring system efficiency and longevity. By scheduling routine inspections and cleanings, facilities can prevent costly breakdowns and maintain optimal performance levels.

Our maintenance services include thorough cleaning, system checks, and real-time monitoring, which not only extend the life of HVAC systems but also improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency, ultimately leading to cost savings for facility managers.

FAQ

About our work

A few of the questions we are asked most often. The full list lives on our home page.

How do you measure a steam coil?
Measuring a steam coil involves assessing its dimensions, fin spacing, and surface area to determine its performance and efficiency for HVAC systems.
How do you measure steam coil dimensions accurately?
Accurately measuring steam coil dimensions involves using a tape measure for length, width, and depth, ensuring precise readings for optimal system fit and performance.
What are the types of steam coil measurement?
Steam coil measurement types include thermal imaging for temperature distribution and ultrasonic testing for detecting internal blockages and fouling.
What tools measure steam coil effectiveness?
Steam coil effectiveness is measured using tools like thermal imaging cameras and airflow sensors to assess heat transfer and air velocity.
Can I measure steam coil efficiency myself?
Measuring steam coil efficiency yourself is challenging. Professional assessment by Steira Air ensures accurate evaluation and optimal performance.
What data is needed for steam coil measurement?
For steam coil measurement, we need data on coil dimensions, steam pressure, temperature, and flow rate for accurate assessment.
How does Steira Air clean steam coils?
Steira Air cleans steam coils using an eco-friendly, high-pressure steam cleaning process that effectively removes contaminants and restores efficiency.
What is air monitoring equipment?
Air monitoring equipment refers to devices used to measure and track the quality of indoor air, detecting pollutants and ensuring a healthy environment.
When should air monitoring be conducted?
Air monitoring should be conducted routinely, during occupancy, or when air quality concerns arise to ensure a healthy environment.
How do you calibrate air monitoring equipment?
Calibrating air monitoring equipment ensures accuracy. Follow manufacturer guidelines for regular calibration using certified calibration gases for precise readings.
