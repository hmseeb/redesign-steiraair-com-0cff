Healthy workplaces

Air quality your occupants can feel

Indoor Air Quality refers to the condition of the air within buildings and structures, especially as it relates to the health and comfort of building occupants. Poor IAQ can lead to respiratory problems, allergies and other serious illnesses.

Steira Air employs state-of-the-art monitoring systems and cleaning technologies to ensure that commercial environments remain safe and conducive to health — and that facility teams have the data to prove it.

  • Real-time PM2.5, VOC and CO2 visibility across the building
  • Chemical-free cleaning that is safe for occupied spaces
  • Eco-friendly methods that support LEED certification goals
