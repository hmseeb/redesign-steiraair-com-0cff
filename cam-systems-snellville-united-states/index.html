What CAM System does for your system

Steira Air's CAM System stands at the pinnacle of innovation in the Indoor Air Quality Monitoring and Air Cleaning industry. This system exemplifies relentless dedication to ensuring the safest, cleanest, and most breathable indoor air possible.

It does two jobs at once: it measures what is actually in your air, and it treats it. Get instant notifications on your app or dashboard for changes in PM2.5, VOCs, CO2 and more, while advanced ion technology neutralizes airborne contaminants without harsh chemicals.
