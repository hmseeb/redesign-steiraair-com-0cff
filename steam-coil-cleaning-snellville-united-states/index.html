What Steam Coil Cleaning does for your system

Steira Air's robust, medium pressure, high temperature steam process completely penetrates thick coils and removes microbial and biofilm growth lodged deep within the coil. Surface cleaning only treats the first few rows of fins — steam reaches the core, where the resistance to airflow and heat transfer actually builds up.

Because the process uses high-temperature steam rather than chemicals, it is safe for occupants and ideal for sustainability-focused facilities. It restores heat transfer efficiency, reduces system strain, extends coil lifespan, and integrates seamlessly with your preventive maintenance schedule.

  • Key benefit: Deep steam for heavy debris
  • Ideal for: Industrial, Retail, Residential, Commercial and Government facilities

The difference Steam Coil Cleaning makes

Chemical-free technology

Proven performance

Full-depth penetration
