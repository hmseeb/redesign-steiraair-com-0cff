What Air Cooled Chiller Steam Cleaning does for your system

Steira Air is the ideal choice for air-cooled chiller cleaning. Due to our expertise, advanced technology, and exceptional results, facilities that cannot afford downtime trust us with the equipment that keeps them running.

Air-cooled chiller condensers sit outdoors and collect everything the environment throws at them. Steam cleaning lifts that load out of the coil face and helps prevent the scaling and fouling that force the compressor to work harder than it should.

  • Key benefit: Scaling prevention
  • Ideal for: Data Centers, Hospitals, Residential, Commercial and Government facilities

Why it works

The difference Air Cooled Chiller Steam Cleaning makes

Restore rejection capacity

Clean condenser coils reject heat the way they were designed to, which keeps head pressure and energy draw where they belong.

Scaling prevention

Regular steam cleaning helps prevent the scale and fouling that quietly erode chiller performance between service intervals.

Built for critical facilities

Ideal for data centers, hospitals, commercial buildings and government facilities where cooling cannot be interrupted.

Chemical-free process

High-temperature steam does the work, so there is no chemical runoff to capture or neutralize on site.
