What Preventive Maintenance does for your system

Regular HVAC maintenance is crucial for ensuring system efficiency and longevity. By scheduling routine inspections and cleanings, facilities can prevent costly breakdowns and maintain optimal performance levels.

Steira Air's maintenance services include thorough cleaning, system checks, and real-time monitoring, which not only extend the life of HVAC systems but also improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency, ultimately leading to cost savings for facility managers.

  • Key benefit: Prevents costly breakdowns
  • Ideal for: Commercial, Government, Healthcare, Industrial and Residential facilities

Why it works

The difference Preventive Maintenance makes

Routine inspections and cleaning

Scheduled service keeps coils, ducts and equipment in the condition they were specified to operate in.

Real-time monitoring included

CMMS and CAM System data turn maintenance from a calendar exercise into a condition-based one.

Lower operating cost

Clean, well-maintained systems draw less energy and last longer, which shows up directly in facility budgets.

Fewer emergency calls

Catching problems early means fewer unplanned shutdowns and fewer uncomfortable days for occupants.
