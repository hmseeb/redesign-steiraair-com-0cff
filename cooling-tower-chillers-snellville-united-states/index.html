Central plant

What Cooling Tower & Chillers does for your system

Cooling towers and chillers are the heart of a central plant, and they are also the equipment most exposed to fouling. Towers pull outdoor air and everything in it across a wet surface; air-cooled chillers bake in the sun collecting debris on the condenser face.

Steira Air brings the same chemical-free steam capability used on coils to this equipment, so heat rejection surfaces stay clean, scaling is kept in check and the plant does not have to work against itself to hold setpoint.

  • Key benefit: Reliable central plant performance
  • Ideal for: Commercial, Industrial, Government and Healthcare facilities
  • Service area: Atlanta, GA · Dallas, TX · nationwide

Why it works

The difference Cooling Tower & Chillers makes

Efficient heat rejection

Clean surfaces mean the plant holds setpoint without the extra compressor and fan energy that fouling demands.

Scaling and fouling control

Regular cleaning keeps mineral scale and biological fouling from establishing on heat transfer surfaces.

Equipment protection

Lower operating pressures and temperatures reduce strain on compressors and extend the usable life of expensive plant.

Maintenance you can measure

Combine with CMMS monitoring to see the effect of each service rather than assuming it.
